BlinkDRS Controller 1.2.26 - Concurrent reference cache
Updated: October 17, 2026

Live verification of the two-worker optimization exposed a previously serial
reference-cache assumption. Two workers can both observe a missing variants key,
compute it independently, and then attempt to insert the same primary key.
The second insertion raised UNIQUE constraint failed: variants.key.

All three cache-writing paths now use INSERT OR IGNORE and read back the stored
row. The first committed result wins, both workers use that result, and existing
cached vectors/quality are never overwritten. Recognition rules and sampling are
unchanged. The 1.2.25 CPU/thread/worker benchmark preference remains applicable.

Validation: 57 regressions passed. Three new barrier-controlled cold-cache tests
cover ordinary variants, manual crop teachers and reviewed examples. All three
reproduce the exact duplicate-key failure against the original implementation
and pass against the fix. Warm caches are also checked for identical output and
no recomputation. A separate spawned-process check verifies one persisted row
and identical results across both processes.

The affected live clip 26366 and work interrupted for this correction were
queued again. The two older unrelated failed jobs were preserved. Deployment checks idle state,
backs up affected files, restores the tested two-worker unit and verifies health,
release metadata, two active slots and stable failure count after startup.

The preceding full-clip test completed both default-sampled clips (17 and 18
frames) and verified all confirmed detections/evidence, profiles, teaching samples
and evaluation holdout rows unchanged. Final cache tests use isolated catalogs.
