Changes
- Reuse high-confidence Person proposals from the existing offline detail model. New Person tracks require at least two sampled frames and native crops at least 48 pixels across. Existing Person detection and identity crops stay intact; independently supported full-body regions help suppress animal-shaped human-part proposals.

The crop check requires a Dog-favoring reference score and margin, support from two distinct confirmed Clips/images, a high-confidence Dog result occupying the crop, and a clear lead over Cat/Person alternatives. At most four additional crop checks run per Clip.
